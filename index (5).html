App icon

An iPhone web app can't change its Home Screen icon by itself (only native apps can). Trick that avoids deleting the app: save the icon below, then in the Shortcuts app create a shortcut “Open URL” with your Jarvis address, tap ⓘ → Add to Home Screen, tap the icon and choose the saved photo. To change it later, just edit that shortcut's icon.

Install on Windows (Opera, Chrome, Edge)

Open this same address in Opera (or Chrome/Edge) on your PC, then use the browser's menu — in Opera: the "..." menu or the icon at the right of the address bar → “Install Jarvis”. It installs as its own app window, with the current theme colour as its icon (no manual trick needed, unlike iPhone). Voice and lecture recording work the same way, using your PC's microphone.

How Jarvis talks to you

Navigation

Off: Jarvis is the home screen and you change screen by talking to him (“open my notes”, “show me the modes”). Settings stays at the bottom right.

Location & weather

Jarvis only uses your position when you ask about the weather or directions, and only keeps an approximate area (about 1 km) on this phone as a fallback. Without it you can still ask about a named city.

Always listening

While Jarvis is open, on any tab, saying “Jarvis” brings you back here and he answers. The screen stays on. Safari can't listen when the screen is locked or you are in another app (iPhone rule). How to get a Client ID (free, 3 minutes)
1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in. 2. Create app: any name, paste the Redirect URI above, tick “Web API”. 3. Open the app's Settings: copy the Client ID here. 4.
